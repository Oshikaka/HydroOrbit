ERA5-DAYMET stores 365 steps in every year (days_of_year = 1…365 even in 1980). Its labels alone can't tell whether step 60 of a leap year is Feb 29 or Mar 1. The point cloud is a continuous Gregorian hourly axis whose weather forcing and streamflow are NaN on Dec 31 of every leap year (the 288 "missing" weather hours in observation_coverage.csv). The tests below decide the mapping empirically, using day-to-day changes that only line up when the days match.

A daily value covers some 24-hour window. For every gauge, the point cloud's hourly series was aggregated over windows starting s hours from 00 UTC of the labelled day (s = −30…+30), and correlated with the ERA5-DAYMET value. The best s is that field's day boundary. ERA5 fields sit on the UTC day. Daymet fields (tmax, tmin, precipitation) are best matched by windows starting 8–13 h before UTC midnight, so pairing them with UTC days misplaces about half a day. For forecasting, a Daymet value for day D is also not known before day D ends.

Are the "Daymet" fields independent of ERA5?

ERA5-DAYMET labels tmax, tmin and precipitation as Daymet. If they were station-based Daymet, they would differ from a reanalysis in fine spatial detail, especially precipitation over mountains.

Read before use

Do not use

sea_surface_temperature is a copy of 2 m temperature

In both gridded datasets every cell, land and sea, equals 2m_temperature exactly, and the normalizer files give both the same μ/σ. It adds no information.

Units

Precipitation is in metres, over different windows

ERA5 total_precipitation is a 1 h total and total_precipitation_24hr a trailing 24 h total. ERA5-DAYMET is a daily total. CAMELSH is a 1 h total. Multiply by 1000 for mm.

Units

CAMELSH radiation is hourly energy

surface_*_radiation_downwards is stored in J m⁻² per hour (NLDAS-2 W m⁻² × 3600), matching ERA5. Divide by 3600 for W m⁻².

Units

ERA5 orography is geopotential

ERA5 0.25° orography reaches 56,424, i.e. m² s⁻²; divide by 9.80665 for metres. ERA5-DAYMET orography reaches 4,001, so it is already in metres.

Calendar

Gridded years drop Dec 31 in leap years

ERA5 0.25° has 8,760 hours per year (1980 runs to 1980-12-30T23, Feb 29 present); ERA5-DAYMET has 365 days. Step k is Jan 1 + k in both. CAMELSH is continuous, but its weather forcing is NaN for all of Dec 31 in leap years.

Timing

Daymet fields are not UTC days

In ERA5-DAYMET, tmax, tmin and precipitation come from Daymet and match 24 h windows starting about 8, 11 and 13 h before 00 UTC. The other fields are ERA5 UTC days. See the Day boundaries tab; aligned_loader.py applies the offsets.

Coverage

Streamflow at 5,767 of 9,008 basins

Median record length 26.0 years (10th–90th percentile 7.1–34.7). Water temperature exists at 1,296 basins (median 8.7 years). Weather forcing is complete at every basin.

Clean in loader

CAMELSH observations contain impossible values

Streamflow is negative in 0.1% of valid hours (min -283 m³ s⁻¹). Water temperature is below 0 °C in 0.8% of valid hours and ranges -7.933 to 59.8 °C (p99 30 °C). Clip or mask these before training. Negative potential evaporation (13.7% of hours) is expected: NLDAS-2 uses it for condensation.

Naming

CAMELSH static names do not match their units

sand_frac, silt_frac, clay_frac, frac_forest, frac_developed and baseflow_index are percentages (0–100); frac_snow is 0–1. HydroATLAS keeps its ×10 encodings for slp_dg_uav, lka_pc_use and dor_pc_pva (max 266.6, 327.8, 8,077).

Structure

Minor file quirks

Both gridded datasets carry a duplicate key lattitude (typo, same as latitude). ERA5-DAYMET's days_of_year, time_of_day and year are shaped (365, 1, 360, 180), with lat/lon swapped relative to the data; harmless because they are constant in space.

How the statistics were computed

Gridded data. Mean and std are area-weighted by cos(latitude). Percentiles come from a cos(latitude)-weighted random sample. Min and max cover every cell read. "Land" means land_sea_mask ≥ 0.5; "zero %" is the share of valid values exactly equal to 0. ERA5-DAYMET: every day and cell, 1980–2021. ERA5 0.25° (~130 TB) is sampled: years 1979, 1988, 1997, 2006, 2015, 2020; all 20 shards per year; 3 hourly steps per shard with rotating hour of day (360 global fields per variable). "CONUS box, land" uses the ERA5-DAYMET lat/lon box for a like-for-like comparison. CAMELSH: every valid (hour, basin) value enters mean, std, min and max; percentiles use a 2×10⁻⁴ random sample. The last gridded column repeats normalize_mean.npz / normalize_std.npz, the unweighted values used for training normalization.
